# optical softness

- polarity: unipolar
- range: low to high
- confidence: 0.64

## Definition
Overall lens-like softening: reduced bite, slight highlight spread, and melted fine detail from the optical path.

## Why it matters
Increasing optical softness while holding subject, pose, framing, and key direction should melt fine detail and gently spread highlight cores without requiring a grain, color, or lighting-ratio change.

## Observable effects
- pores and fabric weave melt
- specular cores widen slightly
- edges lose bite
- image feels lens-like rather than sharpened-digital

## Not the same as
- [vec_diffusion](vec_diffusion.md): Diffusion is the filter or atmosphere mechanism. Optical softness is the observed image property.
- [vec_edge_softness](vec_edge_softness.md): Edge softness is contour width only. Optical softness also changes highlight cores and micro-detail.
- [vec_halation](vec_halation.md): Halation is a colored or bright bleed around lights, not a global melt.
- [vec_telecine_softness](vec_telecine_softness.md): Telecine softness is a transfer-path bandwidth loss, often flatter and more video-like.
- [vec_veiling_glare](vec_veiling_glare.md): Veiling glare lifts contrast with a haze. Optical softness can exist with contrast intact.

## Scoring guidance
0.00 clinical crisp, 0.50 moderate lens softness, 1.00 heavy optical melt.

## Prompt phrases
- low: clinically sharp optical path, high acutance, crisp micro-detail, tight highlight cores
- medium: moderate vintage optical softness, slight highlight spread, natural fine-detail loss
- high: heavy optical softness, melted fine detail, diffused highlight cores, lens-like bloom of definition
