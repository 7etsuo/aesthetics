diffusion

provisional vec_diffusion conf 0.67 optical character

A scattering layer that veils the image, often from filters or atmosphere.

What it changes

Increasing diffusion adds a veil and lifts contrast slightly while edge width stays closer to the source than in an optical-softness sweep.

  • whites and midtones gain a veil
  • contrast lowers slightly
  • lights spread into a mist

Poles

Low: no diffusion veil

High: heavy diffusion veil

Aliases: none

Commonly confused with

  • vec_edge_softness: Edge softness is contour width. Diffusion is a field veil.
  • vec_optical_softness: Optical softness melts definition. Diffusion is a scattering veil that can sit on still-edged forms.
  • vec_veiling_glare: Veiling glare is internal lens scatter that fogs blacks. Diffusion is a filter or atmosphere layer.
