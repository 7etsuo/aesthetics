edge softness

provisional vec_edge_softness conf 0.36 spatial definition

Width and melt of edges, independent of highlight glow.

What it changes

Increasing edge softness thickens and dissolves contours without requiring a veil, highlight spread, or background disk melt.

  • contours thicken
  • hair and fabric edges lose snap
  • highlight cores stay relatively tight

Poles

Low: hard razor edges

High: melted wide edges

Aliases: soft edges, edge melt

Commonly confused with

  • vec_bokeh_softness: Bokeh softness is the out-of-focus field. Edge softness can sit on an in-focus subject.
  • vec_diffusion: Diffusion is a veil over the whole field. Edge softness is contour width.
  • vec_optical_softness: Optical softness includes highlight spread and micro-detail melt, not only edge width.
